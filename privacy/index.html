Privacy Policy

DreamUp Adventure (升夢大冒險)

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · 最後更新：2026 年 10 月 11 日

English 繁體中文

DreamUp Adventure (升夢大冒險) is published by Risemont Education Limited, a company in Hong Kong ("we", "us"), under the CantonRock brand. We built it as a safe, ad-free learning companion for young children and their families, including families in Hong Kong and families who now live in the United Kingdom. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. It is written for parents, because the children who play are too young to read a policy.

1. The short version

  • There is no sign-up, login, or account. We do not ask for your name, your child's name, email, phone number, date of birth, or location.
  • We do not show advertisements, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We have no servers of our own. Learning progress stays on your device.
  • The microphone and speech recognition are used only so your child can play. We do not store that audio and we do not send it to ourselves.
  • Voice practice recordings you choose to make in the parent tools stay on your device, and you can delete them at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple, behind a parental gate.
  • The only information that leaves the device for us is a random subscription identifier and limited technical details, sent to RevenueCat so the app can tell whether premium is unlocked.

2. Who we are

Risemont Education Limited, Hong Kong, is the data user under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. The same company is the controller under the UK GDPR for personal data about people in the United Kingdom. The Hong Kong rules apply because we are a Hong Kong company. The United Kingdom section below is additional for people in the UK. It does not replace the Hong Kong rights.

Privacy questions: privacy@risemonteducation.com. App support: support@risemonteducation.com.

We have no office in the United Kingdom. A UK representative is not yet appointed. Details are in the United Kingdom section below.

Any personal data described in this policy is used only to provide the learning activities and, if a parent subscribes, to verify that subscription. Supplying it is voluntary. The app does not need your name. If you turn the microphone and speech recognition off, only the speak-aloud games stop. We do not use personal data for direct marketing, and we do not use it for a new purpose.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store your name, your child's name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifier. We do not build marketing profiles, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

Several games ask your child to read a word or sentence aloud. The app listens through the microphone and uses Apple's Speech Recognition to turn the speech into text, which it compares with the expected answer. Where the device can do that on its own, the audio stays on the device. Where it cannot, Apple may process the audio on Apple's servers. That processing is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not receive that audio, we do not store it, and we do not send it to RevenueCat or to anyone else. You can turn the microphone or speech recognition permission on or off at any time in the iOS Settings app. Turning them off disables the speak-aloud games. The rest of the app keeps working.

The iOS permission screen is a device setting. It is not a request that your child consent to their information being used. We do not ask children to agree to this policy.

5. Practice recordings (parent tools)

In the interview-practice, read-aloud, and picture-story tools, a parent or child can deliberately record a short answer to review later. These recordings:

  • are saved only in the app's private storage on your device;
  • are excluded from iCloud backup, so they do not sync off the device;
  • are never uploaded to us or to any third party;
  • can be deleted at any time within the app, and are removed entirely if you delete the app.

6. Information stored on your device

Learning progress, game settings, parent notes, the practice-reminder switch, and a cached subscription flag are stored locally using standard iOS storage. This information stays on the device and is under your control. The list of words to practise is there so a parent can see what to try again. It is not sent to us, and it is not used to advertise.

Practice reminders are off until a parent turns them on. The app will ask for notification permission only after that choice.

7. Purchases and subscriptions

Optional premium subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see or receive payment-card details. On the UK App Store, Apple shows the price in pounds, VAT included, and Apple is the seller. In Hong Kong, Apple shows the price in Hong Kong dollars.

To unlock and verify premium access, we use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app identifier and the purchase history Apple reports, along with limited technical information such as device model, operating-system version, and App Store country. The country comes from the storefront, not from GPS. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your child's name, or your payment details.

For that subscription information, we decide the purpose and RevenueCat processes it for us. RevenueCat's data-processing terms include the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for transfers from the United Kingdom. Purchases sit behind a parental gate, a short multiplication question, so a child does not buy by accident.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

We keep no separate copy of the purchase record. RevenueCat keeps the identifier for as long as it needs to verify the subscription, as described in its own policy. On-device progress and recordings remain until you delete them or delete the app. We do not keep personal data longer than we need for these purposes.

8. Children and families

DreamUp Adventure is designed for children aged about 4 to 7, used together with a parent. We apply the same high privacy standard to every person who uses the app. We do not ask anyone for a date of birth, because we do not need one: the children's protections are on for everyone.

There are no advertisements and no social features. The screens a child plays do not offer web links. Purchases sit behind a parental gate. This policy, the terms, and support are opened from the parent area.

9. Where processing happens

We do not transfer personal data to servers of our own. We have none. Apple may process payment information and, when on-device recognition is not available, speech audio, on servers outside Hong Kong and outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Apple's privacy policy covers that processing. RevenueCat may process the subscription identifier and the limited technical details in the same way. The classes of persons who may receive that information are Apple and RevenueCat. We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising or direct marketing.

10. Hong Kong

The Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance applies to Risemont Education Limited as a data user in Hong Kong. These rights continue to apply, including when a family uses the app from the United Kingdom.

You may ask whether we hold personal data about you, ask for a copy, and ask for a correction. Email privacy@risemonteducation.com. We do not charge a fee for this, and we will answer within 40 days, as the Ordinance requires. Because we do not hold a file of names or contact details, purchase records usually have to be requested from Apple or RevenueCat. We will help where we can.

You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/.

11. If you are in the United Kingdom

This app is offered to families in the United Kingdom, including families who have moved from Hong Kong. The UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply to personal data about people in the UK, in addition to the Hong Kong protection above. Because children use the app, we also follow the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code): high privacy by default, no advertising, no tracking, and a parental gate on purchases.

The legal basis is to provide the service a parent has asked for: the learning activities, and the subscription if they choose to buy one. That is UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b). In the United Kingdom a child under 13 cannot consent to this kind of service, and we do not rely on a child's consent.

You can ask us what personal data we hold, and you can ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict it, or provide a copy. Because we hold so little ourselves, purchase records usually have to be requested from Apple or RevenueCat. Email privacy@risemonteducation.com and we will help where we can.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

UK representative: not yet appointed. Risemont Education Limited is based in Hong Kong and has no office in the United Kingdom. Until a representative is appointed, email privacy@risemonteducation.com. When the appointment is made, this page will name the representative and give a United Kingdom contact address.

12. Data security

Information stored on your device is protected by iOS's app sandbox and the device's own security. As we do not operate servers or hold a database of users, there is no central file of families to breach. The subscription identifier held by RevenueCat is protected under RevenueCat's security measures.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. The 11 October 2026 update keeps the Hong Kong rights under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and adds the United Kingdom section. Material changes will appear on this page before they take effect.

14. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, or if you want to make an access or correction request, contact:

Risemont Education Limited Privacy: privacy@risemonteducation.com
App support: support@risemonteducation.com
Hong Kong

私隱政策（繁體中文）

DreamUp Adventure（升夢大冒險）由香港公司 Risemont Education Limited 發佈（下稱「我們」），並以 CantonRock 品牌經營。我們希望為兒童及家庭提供一個安全、無廣告的學習夥伴，對象包括香港家庭，以及現居英國的家庭。本政策以淺白文字，說明本應用程式會處理及不會處理哪些資料。政策是寫給家長看的，因為玩遊戲的孩子還未能閱讀這類文字。

1. 重點摘要

  • 無需註冊、登入或建立帳戶。我們不會要求你或孩子的姓名、電郵、電話號碼、出生日期或位置。
  • 沒有廣告，亦沒有第三方分析或追蹤。
  • 我們沒有自己的伺服器。學習進度只留在你的裝置上。
  • 麥克風及語音辨識只用於讓孩子進行遊戲。我們不會儲存該等聲音，亦不會傳送給我們自己。
  • 你在家長工具中主動錄製的練習錄音只會留在裝置上，並可隨時刪除。
  • 購買由 Apple 處理，並設有家長關卡。
  • 唯一為我們而離開裝置的資料，是一個隨機訂閱識別碼及有限的技術資料，傳送給 RevenueCat，以便應用程式知道完整版是否已解鎖。

2. 我們是誰

香港公司 Risemont Education Limited 是《個人資料（私隱）條例》下的資料使用者。同一間公司就英國境內人士的個人資料而言，是英國《一般資料保護規則》下的資料控制者。香港的規則適用，是因為我們是香港公司。下文英國一節是給在英國的人士的額外說明，並不取代香港的權利。

私隱查詢：privacy@risemonteducation.com。應用程式支援：support@risemonteducation.com。

我們在英國沒有辦事處。英國代表尚未委任，詳見下文英國一節。

本政策所述的任何個人資料，只用於提供學習活動，以及在家長訂閱時核對該訂閱。提供與否屬自願。應用程式並不需要你的姓名。若你關閉麥克風及語音辨識，只會停用朗讀類遊戲。我們不會把個人資料用於直接促銷，亦不會用於新目的。

3. 我們不會收集的資料

我們不會收集或儲存你或孩子的姓名、電郵地址、電話號碼、通訊地址、出生日期、精確或概略位置、通訊錄、相片，或任何廣告識別碼。我們不會建立推銷用的用戶檔案，亦不會跨其他應用程式或網站追蹤你。

4. 麥克風及語音辨識

部分遊戲會要求孩子朗讀字詞或句子。為此，本應用程式會透過麥克風聆聽，並使用 Apple 的「語音辨識」將語音轉為文字，再與預期答案比對。若裝置本身能夠辨識，聲音會留在裝置上。若不能，Apple 可能在其伺服器上處理該段聲音。該處理受 Apple 的私隱政策 規管。

我們不會收到該段聲音，不會儲存它，亦不會把它傳送給 RevenueCat 或任何其他人。你可隨時在 iOS「設定」中開啟或關閉麥克風或語音辨識權限。關閉後，朗讀類遊戲將無法使用，應用程式的其他部分仍可正常運作。

iOS 的權限畫面是裝置設定，並非要求孩子同意使用其資料。我們不會要求孩子同意本政策。

5. 練習錄音（家長工具）

在面試練習、朗讀及看圖說話等工具中，家長或孩子可以主動錄製簡短答案，以便日後重溫。這些錄音：

  • 只會儲存在你裝置上本應用程式的私人儲存空間內；
  • 已排除於 iCloud 備份之外，因此不會同步離開裝置；
  • 絕不會上載給我們或任何第三方；
  • 可隨時在應用程式內刪除；若你刪除應用程式，這些錄音亦會被完全移除。

6. 儲存在你裝置上的資料

學習進度、遊戲設定、家長筆記、練習提醒開關，以及暫存的訂閱狀態，均使用標準 iOS 儲存方式，留在裝置本機。這些資料由你控制。需要再練習的字詞清單，是讓家長知道該重溫甚麼。它不會傳送給我們，亦不用作廣告。

練習提醒預設關閉，須由家長開啟。應用程式只會在家長作出這個選擇之後，才詢問通知權限。

7. 購買及訂閱

可選的進階訂閱透過 Apple App Store 銷售。Apple 處理付款。我們不會看到或取得付款卡資料。在英國 App Store，Apple 以英鎊顯示已包含增值稅的價格，並由 Apple 作為銷售方。在香港，Apple 以港元顯示價格。

為解鎖及驗證進階權限，我們使用訂閱管理服務 RevenueCat。RevenueCat 會收到一個隨機產生的應用程式識別碼、Apple 所報告的購買紀錄，以及有限的技術資料（例如裝置型號、作業系統版本及 App Store 國家或地區）。國家或地區來自商店店面，並非來自 GPS。RevenueCat 不會收到你或孩子的姓名，亦不會收到付款資料。

就這些訂閱資料而言，由我們決定使用目的，RevenueCat 代我們處理。RevenueCat 的資料處理條款包含英國《國際資料轉移附錄》，適用於從英國作出的轉移。購買設有家長關卡（一條簡短的乘數題），以免孩子誤觸購買。

  • Apple 私隱政策：https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat 私隱政策：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

我們不會另行保存一份購買紀錄。RevenueCat 會按其私隱政策，在需要核對訂閱的期間保留該識別碼。裝置上的進度及錄音會一直保留，直至你刪除它們或刪除應用程式。我們不會把個人資料保留超過這些目的所需的時間。

8. 兒童及家庭

DreamUp Adventure 是為約 4 至 7 歲、與家長一同使用的兒童而設。我們對每一位用戶採用同一套高私隱標準。我們不會向任何人索取出生日期，因為並不需要：兒童的私隱保護對所有人都生效。

應用程式內沒有廣告，亦沒有社交功能。孩子遊玩的畫面沒有網頁連結。購買設有家長關卡。本政策、使用條款及支援可從家長區開啟。

9. 處理在何處進行

我們不會把個人資料轉移到自己的伺服器，因為我們並沒有伺服器。Apple 可能在香港及英國以外（包括美國）的伺服器上，處理付款資料，以及在裝置無法自行辨識時的語音。該處理受 Apple 的私隱政策規管。RevenueCat 亦可能以同樣方式處理訂閱識別碼及有限的技術資料。可能接收該等資料的類別是 Apple 及 RevenueCat。我們不會出售這些資料，亦不會把它們用於廣告或直接促銷。

10. 香港

《個人資料（私隱）條例》適用於作為香港資料使用者的 Risemont Education Limited。這些權利繼續適用，包括家人在英國使用本應用程式的情況。

你可查詢我們是否持有你的個人資料、要求一份副本，並要求改正。請電郵 privacy@risemonteducation.com。我們不會就此收費，並會按條例的規定在 40 日內回覆。由於我們並不持有一份載有姓名或聯絡資料的檔案，購買紀錄通常須向 Apple 或 RevenueCat 提出。我們會在能力範圍內協助。

你亦可聯絡香港個人資料私隱專員公署：https://www.pcpd.org.hk/。

11. 若你人在英國

本應用程式提供給英國的家庭使用，包括由香港移居英國的家庭。英國《一般資料保護規則》（UK GDPR）及《2018 年資料保護法》在上文的香港保障之外，適用於與英國境內人士有關的個人資料。因為兒童會使用本應用程式，我們同時遵循英國資訊專員辦公室的《適齡設計守則》（Children's Code）：預設高私隱、沒有廣告、沒有追蹤，購買設有家長關卡。

法律依據是履行家長所要求的服務：學習活動，以及家長選擇購買的訂閱。這屬於英國《一般資料保護規則》第 6 條第 1 款 (b) 項所指的合約必要處理。在英國，13 歲以下兒童不能同意使用這類服務，我們亦不依賴孩子的同意。

你可要求我們說明所持有的個人資料，並可要求更正、刪除、限制處理，或提供一份副本。由於我們本身所持資料極少，購買紀錄通常須向 Apple 或 RevenueCat 提出。請電郵 privacy@risemonteducation.com，我們會在能力範圍內協助。

你可向英國資訊專員辦公室（ICO）投訴：https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/。

儲存在裝置上的資料，受 iOS 應用程式沙盒及裝置本身的保安保護。我們並不營運伺服器，亦沒有一個集中的家庭資料庫。RevenueCat 所持的訂閱識別碼，受其保安措施保護。
